# IBM HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores employee attrition patterns using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset. The objective is to identify key factors influencing employee turnover and provide data-driven insights that can help organizations improve employee retention.

The analysis was conducted using data queries and business intelligence techniques to uncover trends related to employee demographics, job roles, income, overtime, job satisfaction, and other workforce characteristics.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across different demographic and job-related factors.
- Identify departments and job roles with high attrition rates.
- Examine the relationship between salary, overtime, and employee turnover.
- Provide actionable recommendations for improving employee retention.

---

## Tools Used

- PostgreSQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query

---

## Dataset

- **Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset
- **Records:** 1,470 employees
- **Source:** IBM Sample HR Analytics Dataset

---


## SQL Queries

The queries include analyses of:

- Employee Attrition by Department
- Employee Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Salary Band
- Attrition by Job Level
- Attrition by Overtime Status
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Business Travel
- Monthly Income Analysis
- Job Satisfaction Analysis

---

## Key Insights

- Employees working overtime showed a significantly higher attrition rate.
- Lower salary bands experienced the highest employee turnover.
- Entry-level employees (Job Level 1) recorded the highest attrition.
- Certain departments and job roles contributed disproportionately to employee exits.
- Employee satisfaction and work-life balance appeared to influence retention.

---

## Recommendations

- Improve employee compensation for lower salary bands.
- Monitor employee workload and reduce excessive overtime.
- Develop targeted retention programs for high-risk departments and job roles.
- Strengthen employee engagement and career development initiatives.
- Conduct regular employee satisfaction surveys to identify emerging concerns.
